# AMSTAR 2 implementation rules

## Current implementation stage

The application currently implements:

- a typed assessment model;
- separate recording of item response, weakness judgement and critical-flaw judgement;
- prespecification of critical domains with rationale;
- mandatory rationale and evidence location;
- validation of all 16 AMSTAR 2 items;
- no numerical total score;
- no automatic overall-confidence algorithm.

## Methodological safeguards

AMSTAR 2 responses must not be added together to create a numerical score.

The seven critical domains proposed in the original publication are items 2, 4, 7, 9, 11, 13 and 15. The publication states that this list is advisory and may be adapted when justified and prespecified for the review context.

A response of Partial Yes is stored separately from the appraiser's judgement about whether the finding constitutes a weakness or a critical flaw.

Any final overall-confidence judgement must include a documented rationale.
